# KNIT WD-0000 · Authority-Aware AI

**Working Draft 0.6.1. Public working draft: requirements and a conformance reporting framework for advisory AI systems that answer from bounded, declared corpora of institutional texts.**

AI systems that answer questions from institutional rules become things people rely on. This working draft is about keeping that reliance from being mistaken for, or replacing, the authority of the institutions whose texts they read.

> Authority-aware AI may reason about institutional authority but must not misrepresent, usurp, or silently assume it.

- **[SPECIFICATION.md](SPECIFICATION.md)**: the draft. Foundations, four assurance layers, a coverage statement, and 17 requirements (R1–R17).
- **[conformance/](conformance/README.md)**: the Authority-Aware AI Conformance Suite (a reporting framework with illustrative fixture specifications; tests not yet built).
- **[evidence/](evidence/implementation-matrix.md)**: implementation-maturity observations for the first implementation, [Open Order](https://github.com/KNIT-NZ/open-order), at a pinned historical commit. They inform the draft; they are not conformance results, and Open Order is not the draft's canonical interpretation.

**Status:** Working Draft 0.6.1, a public working draft for comment. Initial comment window: 26 November to 26 December 2026 (New Zealand dates). The requirements were abstracted from one procedural corpus (the Standing Orders and Speakers' Rulings of the New Zealand House of Representatives). Application to other bounded institutional rule systems is intended; application to legislation, case law and mixed legal research is not demonstrated. Several requirements are provisional (their operational definitions are pending), so no overall conformance claim is currently available, and Structural conformance is proposed, not currently claimable. Not a standard, not an IETF RFC and not peer reviewed. Conformance does not establish legal compliance or confer institutional authority or permission to provide regulated services.
