# Governance

## Stewardship

KNIT stewards this specification for now. The maintainers are the people with merge rights in this repository; at present that is the author.

The maintainers:
- run the comment and change-proposal process in CONTRIBUTING.md;
- decide on proposed changes, and record decisions and reasons publicly;
- keep the specification separate from any single implementation, including KNIT's own (SPECIFICATION.md §1.3);
- publish versioned releases and their archived records.

## Independence from implementations

Open Order is KNIT's software and informs this specification, but it has no special standing under it. A change to the specification is not justified by the fact that Open Order already does something, and Open Order is assessed against the specification in the same way as any other implementation (evidence/).

## Funding and control

No funder, partner or institution acquires control over the specification's content, or over conformance conclusions, by providing support. Material funding that relates to this work will be disclosed in this file.

## Transfer of stewardship

A neutral, multi-party home for the specification may eventually be appropriate. That is deferred until there is independent participation to transfer it to: implementers, reviewers or institutions outside KNIT who are actively involved. Until then, a commitment to transfer would be premature. The conditions and process for any transfer will be proposed as a change proposal and go through the ordinary comment period.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance follow the change-proposal process in CONTRIBUTING.md.
